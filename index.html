The story

A community driven road to Spain

Matt French and Navarro are heading to Jerez to represent Team USA in Working Equitation. This campaign is being carried by friends, family, and the horse community.

This journey is being driven by a village.
Every donation, share, message, and bit of encouragement helps Matt and Navarro make the trip to Spain and compete on the world stage.
  • First U.S. team to compete at the Working Equitation World Championship
  • San Diego-based horse-and-rider team
  • Supporting international transport, horse care, and travel logistics
Budget

A transparent $50,000 first goal.
